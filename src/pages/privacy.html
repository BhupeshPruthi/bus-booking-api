Balaji Sevak

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 October 2026

The Balaji Sevak app for iPhone and Android is published by Shri Sankat Mochan Balaji Mandal (Regd.), Rani Bagh, Delhi (“we”, “us”). The app helps devotees book bus yatras, puja tokens and Dharamshala stays for Shri Mehandipur Balaji, and keep a Hanuman Chalisa count. This policy explains what information the app collects, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

Your account

When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name, your email address and an account identifier from Google or Apple. If you choose Apple’s “Hide My Email”, we receive a private relay address instead of your email. If you add a phone number, we store it too.

Your bookings

  • Bus: passenger name and phone number, number of seats, trip and pickup point.
  • Puja token: name, phone number, city and number of members.
  • Dharamshala stay: contact name, email and phone number, number of guests, dates, rooms, any coupon code and any note you add.
  • Cancellations: your cancellation requests and any reason you give.

Hanuman Chalisa counter

The name you enter, and the number and time of the rounds you count. If you use the counter without signing in, the app keeps a random guest key on your device so it can keep your count.

Feedback

Messages you send us from the Help screen.

Technical information

Our servers keep standard logs, such as IP address, the request made and the time, to keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment card details. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To process and confirm your bookings, and to contact you about them.
  • To keep your Hanuman Chalisa count and show the group total and leaderboard.
  • To reply to your feedback and support requests.
  • To keep the service secure and working properly.

What other people can see

When you join a group Hanuman Chalisa Path, your display name and count are shown to other app users on the leaderboard. Please choose a name you are comfortable sharing. Your bookings are visible only to you and to the members of our team who manage them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • with members of our team who manage buses, pujas and the Dharamshala, so they can handle your booking;
  • with service providers that run the app for us, such as our cloud hosting and database provider, who may only use it to provide that service;
  • with Google or Apple when you choose to sign in with them (their own privacy policies apply);
  • when we are required to by law.

Payments

The app does not take payments. Any payment for a booking is made directly to us outside the app.

Live events

The Live events screen shows a web page from bolguru.com inside the app. That website’s own terms and privacy practices apply to it.

How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your account information while your account is active. You can ask us to delete your account at any time by emailing bolguru03@gmail.com from the email address linked to your account. Where the app offers it, you can also delete your account from the Profile screen.

When your account is deleted, we remove your name, email address, phone number and sign-in identifiers, and your Hanuman Chalisa counts. We may keep records of past bookings, including the details entered for them, where we need them for our accounts or the law requires it. Server logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. You can stop using the Lock Screen counter at any time from the Hanuman Chalisa screen or your phone’s settings.

Security

The app talks to our servers over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and we limit access to your information to the people who need it. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Children

The app is not directed at children. If you are under 18, please use it with the permission of a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has given us personal information without that permission, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
